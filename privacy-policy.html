Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026 · AGFHUB, a service operated by IP Independent Services INC ("we", "us", "our")

This Privacy Policy explains how AGFHUB collects, uses, and protects information when you use our home-care platform and when you receive text messages (SMS) from us.

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect: your name, mobile phone number, email address, and information you submit through onboarding or scheduling forms. If you are a patient or caregiver, we may also handle health-related information solely to provide care coordination services, which we protect in accordance with applicable law (including HIPAA where it applies).

How we use your information

We use your information to operate the platform, coordinate scheduling and visits, complete onboarding and compliance tasks, and send you text messages and notifications (for example: job application and onboarding links, appointment and shift reminders, and account notifications).

Text messaging (SMS)

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors who provide support services (such as our messaging platform) is permitted only so we can deliver the messages you have requested. All other categories exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How you opt in to SMS messages

You opt in to receive text messages from AGFHUB by submitting your mobile number on our online sign-up form at https://agfhub-site.onrender.com/opt-in.html and checking the optional SMS consent box(es), or by providing verbal or written consent to agency staff during onboarding. SMS consent is optional, is collected separately from any other agreement, and is not a condition of employment or of using our services.

Types of messages and your controls

If you opt in, we send transactional messages (job application and onboarding links, appointment and shift reminders, and account notifications) and, if you separately opt in, occasional promotional messages about new services. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and you can get help by replying HELP or contacting us at the details below.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the platform (such as hosting, database, and messaging providers), and only to the extent needed to provide the service. We may also disclose information where required by law. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share mobile opt-in data with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and tracking

Our public website and policy pages do not use advertising cookies or third-party marketing trackers. Within the AGFHUB platform we may use essential cookies and similar technologies that are necessary to sign you in, keep your session secure, and remember basic preferences. We do not use your browsing information for third-party advertising, and we do not sell it. Most browsers let you control or delete cookies through their settings; blocking essential cookies may limit parts of the platform.

Data security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data using industry-standard measures.

Your choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and operational limits.
