You opt in by providing your mobile number during onboarding or scheduling, or by texting us to start. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or of employment.

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you reply STOP, we will send one confirmation message and then stop sending SMS to you.
